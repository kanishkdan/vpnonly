A switch per app

Every app on your Mac, searchable in the menu bar. Flip one on — it relaunches inside the tunnel with a new exit IP. Flip it off — it's back home.

Kill switch, always

Tunnel drops? That app's traffic is blocked, not quietly leaked to your ISP. Reconnect and it's back — no surprises in between.

Bring any VPN

NordVPN works out of the box — paste a token once. Or drop in a WireGuard key from Mullvad, Proton, IVPN, or your own server.

One password, once

Admin rights are needed to make firewall rules — that's macOS. VPNonly asks a single time, installs a root-owned engine, and never asks again.

Auditable core

Every privileged line — tunnel, firewall, launcher — is ~300 lines of open-source shell and C on GitHub. No opaque binary gets root.

Native and tiny

A real Mac menu bar app — no Electron, no accounts, no analytics. Your WireGuard key never leaves your machine.
