hi, this is vpnonly.

it's a mac app i made for myself.

nordvpn — like basically every big vpn — has no split tunneling on mac. it's all or nothing: connect, and your entire machine moves to another country. video calls get worse, your bank gets suspicious, downloads slow down. i just wanted capcut on a singapore ip while everything else stayed exactly where it was.

so: pick an app, flip a switch, that app is on the vpn. everything else on your mac never notices. flip it back whenever you want.

works with nordvpn out of the box.

what it actually does

it opens a wireguard tunnel that nothing uses by default — your normal internet is untouched, your default route is never changed. then it uses the firewall that's already in macos to send just the apps you picked through that tunnel.

the first time an app joins, it gets relaunched once (macos decides an app's network identity when it starts, so there's no way around that). after that, switching it in and out is instant.

if the tunnel ever drops, those apps get blocked rather than quietly falling back to your normal connection. the worst thing that can happen is one app loses internet — never your mac.

things i should be honest about

  • apple silicon only for now.
  • dns lookups for tunneled apps still go through your normal resolver. the connections themselves are tunneled. if you need to hide which sites you visit from your isp, this isn't the tool yet.
  • switching an app's route drops its open connections — most apps reconnect on their own, but a big upload would restart.
  • it's one person maintaining this. if it doesn't work for you, email me and i'll refund you.

why it's paid but also open

the split-tunneling engine is ~300 lines of shell and c, mit licensed, on github. it runs as root on your machine, so you should be able to read every line of it — that's not a favor, it's the minimum.

the $19 is for the mac app on top: the menu bar thing, the app list, one-click setup, updates. if you'd rather use the terminal, do that, it's free and always will be.
