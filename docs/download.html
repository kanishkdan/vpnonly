Updating from an older version

Quit VPNonly (click the shield, then Quit), drag this one into Applications replacing the old one, and open it. Your licence key, VPN settings and app choices are all kept.

This is the last time you'll have to do that. From 1.5.0 onwards VPNonly updates itself: you get a prompt, click Install, and it handles the rest without macOS asking you to approve the app again.

What's new

  • Your router, printer and NAS stay reachable. Apps on the VPN can reach your own network while everything else still goes through the tunnel. It's on by default, as Allow Local Network in the ⋯ menu.
  • Browsers keep their tabs. Switching on a browser that's open quits it the way ⌘Q does and brings it back with its tabs.
  • It tells you when something's off. The popup says when no app is on the VPN yet, and warns when AdGuard or a proxy on your Mac would send apps around it.
  • Check every app. One click asks each app on the VPN which address it really uses.
