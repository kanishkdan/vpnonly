You're in. Here's the setup.

Thanks for buying VPNonly. It's an early product and you're one of the first. Setup is four steps, about two minutes. If any of it goes wrong, email me and I'll either fix it or refund you.

1. Get the download and your licence key

Both are in the email from Dodo Payments that just landed. The key looks like 1a2b3c4d-…. Keep that email: it's your receipt too, and the download link stays valid.

Nothing arrived, or you've lost it? Check spam first, then open your purchases page and sign in with the email you bought with. Your download and licence key are both there. Still stuck? Email me.

2. Install it

Unzip, drag VPNonly.app to your Applications folder.

macOS blocks it the first time, because VPNonly isn't signed with an Apple developer certificate yet. To get past that:

  1. Double-click VPNonly. macOS refuses: "Apple could not verify VPNonly is free of malware." Click Done.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security.
  3. Next to the line about VPNonly, click Open Anyway and authenticate.
  4. Double-click VPNonly again, click Open. Done forever.

Don't skip step 1. The "Open Anyway" button only appears after macOS has blocked an attempt. If it still doesn't show, run this in Terminal and open the app normally:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VPNonly.app

3. Paste your licence key

The shield appears in your menu bar. Click it, paste the key from your email, hit Activate. Your licence covers 2 Macs, so you can do this again on a second machine later.

Can't see the shield? Your menu bar is probably full. On Macs with a notch, macOS hides icons that don't fit. Quit a menu bar app you're not using and it'll appear.

4. Connect your VPN

NordVPN: get an access token at my.nordaccount.com → Manual configuration → Access token, paste it into VPNonly once. It fetches your key and handles servers from there. Keep the NordVPN app closed while you use VPNonly.

Mullvad, Proton, IVPN, AirVPN, or your own server: download a WireGuard .conf from your provider (one per server) and choose Import WireGuard config…. Import as many as you like and switch between them from the menu at the bottom.

That's it. Flip a switch.

Every app on your Mac is in the list. Flip one on and it joins the VPN. If it's already open, VPNonly restarts it once and warns you first, so save your work. After that, switching is instant. Your choice is remembered, so next time you open that app it goes into the VPN on its own.

Everything else on your Mac stays exactly where it was.

Two things worth knowing

  • Connections are tunneled, but DNS lookups still go through your normal resolver. That's fine for changing an app's exit IP, but not enough if you need to hide which sites you visit from your ISP.
  • To remove everything later, use the … button and choose Uninstall VPNonly. That removes the root-owned engine and its permissions properly, not just the app.
