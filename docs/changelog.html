Changelog

Everything that's changed, including the things that were broken. VPNonly updates itself from 1.5.0 onwards.

Working alongside everything else · 1.9.8–1.9.10 · August 2026

  • It works when another VPN is already running. VPNonly used to assume a particular tunnel was its own. If something else had taken it, VPNonly would report a connection it didn't have. It now asks macOS for a free one and proves the tunnel is its own before claiming anything.
  • It no longer disturbs your other firewall rules. Its rules live in their own space rather than being mixed into the system's, so other tools keep working and VPNonly stops clobbering them.
  • Connected means connected. The status only says so after the VPN server has actually answered.
  • It asks before closing an app. A background check could previously quit a running app with no warning, which cost people their browser tabs.
  • A blocked app fails straight away instead of hanging until it times out.

Showing its work · 1.9.0–1.9.7 · August 2026

  • Check. A button that asks the internet what a routed app's exit IP actually is, next to your Mac's own, so you can see it working instead of taking my word for it. It re-checks in the background too.
  • Switching an app out is instant. Its connections used to keep using the tunnel for up to a minute.
  • Safari is gone from the list. It can't be routed by any per-app VPN on macOS, so offering a switch that always refuses was worse than not offering it.
  • Release This Mac. Hand an activation back so your licence can move to another machine.
  • Open it in a window, instead of the menu bar panel, and it remembers which you prefer.
  • Updates arrive within the hour. Fixes for launching after a reinstall, and for apps appearing closed after an update.

Every country, and staying put · 1.5.0–1.6.5 · August 2026

  • Every country your provider offers — 149, not a hardcoded ten — with search and recents.
  • Apps stay in the VPN. Quitting an app used to quietly take it out, so opening it again put it back on your normal connection. The switch is now a lasting choice.
  • Automatic updates, installed with one click without macOS asking you to approve the app again.
  • No more vanishing into a full menu bar: VPNonly opens in a window with a Dock icon instead. Multi-monitor and licence field fixes.

Any provider, and instant switching · 1.2.0–1.4.0 · August 2026

  • Any WireGuard provider. Import a config from Mullvad, Proton, IVPN, AirVPN or your own server, alongside NordVPN token setup. Import as many as you like and switch between them.
  • Instant switching. Each app gets its own private group, so moving one in or out is a firewall change rather than a restart. An app only restarts the first time it joins.
  • The menu bar app: every app on your Mac, searchable, with a switch each, and one password prompt ever instead of one per action.
  • Licence activation, and a proper uninstaller that removes the root-owned engine and its permissions rather than leaving them behind. Firewall rules are validated before they load and can only ever target VPNonly's own groups.
