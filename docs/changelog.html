Changelog

Everything that's changed, including the things that were broken. VPNonly updates itself from 1.5.0 onwards.

1.8.1 · 19 August 2026

  • Corrects the uninstall note, and keeps the app's icon on the dialogs shown while it removes itself.

1.8.0 · 18 August 2026

  • Adds a Check button that asks the internet what a routed app's exit IP actually is, next to your Mac's own — so you can see it working rather than take my word for it.

1.7.11 · 18 August 2026

  • Switching an app out of the VPN now restores its connection immediately instead of after a wait, and Safari no longer appears in the list since it can't be routed.

1.7.10 · 18 August 2026

  • The licence field stays on one line when you paste a key copied out of an email.

1.7.9 · 18 August 2026

  • Signs the bundled WireGuard binaries properly, so the app's signature verifies.

1.7.8 · 18 August 2026

  • The download is now just VPNonly.app instead of a folder called dist, and moving it to Applications removes the copy you downloaded instead of leaving two.

1.7.7 · 18 August 2026

  • Fixes not being able to click into the licence field, and adds the window button to the licence screen.

1.7.6 · 18 August 2026

  • Uninstall now moves VPNonly to the Trash for you instead of asking you to do it.

1.7.5 · 18 August 2026

  • Fixes the menu bar panel rendering see-through.

1.7.4 · 17 August 2026

  • Uninstall no longer fails when there was nothing installed to remove.

1.7.3 · 17 August 2026

  • Fixes the move-to-Applications prompt appearing twice, and moving now works when a copy is already installed.

1.7.2 · 17 August 2026

  • Adds a button to pop the app out into a window you can move and keep open, and back to the menu bar again. It remembers which you prefer.

1.7.1 · 17 August 2026

  • The menu now opens under the icon on the display you're using, instead of landing on another monitor.

1.7.0 · 17 August 2026

  • Refuses to route Safari and explains why, offers to move itself to Applications on first launch, and adds Release This Mac so a licence can move to another machine.

1.6.5 · 17 August 2026

  • Opens on the display you're using, and no longer clips the window on multi-monitor Macs.

1.6.4 · 17 August 2026

  • Opens on the display you're using, and no longer clips the window on multi-monitor Macs.

1.6.3 · 17 August 2026

  • Clears the licence field once your key is accepted.

1.6.2 · 17 August 2026

  • Fixes a second window appearing on multi-monitor Macs, and the licence field not syncing between windows.

1.6.1 · 16 August 2026

  • Points the in-app buy link at the new store.

1.6.0 · 14 August 2026

  • Every country your provider offers (149, not a hardcoded ten), with search and recents. Plus a what's-new note after each update.

1.5.0 · 14 August 2026

  • Apps stay in the VPN. Quitting an app used to quietly take it out of the VPN, so opening it again put it back on your normal connection. Now the switch is a lasting choice: open the app from anywhere and it rejoins the tunnel on its own.
  • No more vanishing into the menu bar. If the menu bar is full, macOS hides the newest icon without saying so, and VPNonly looked like it had failed to launch. It now opens in a window with a Dock icon instead.
  • Automatic updates. New versions install with one click, without macOS asking you to approve the app again.
  • Double-clicking VPNonly while it's already running no longer shows "not responding" — it opens the window.

1.4.0 · 4 August 2026

  • Any WireGuard provider. Import a config from Mullvad, Proton, IVPN, AirVPN or your own server, alongside the existing NordVPN token setup. Import as many servers as you like and switch between them.
  • Licence activation, and a proper uninstaller that removes the root-owned engine and its permissions rather than leaving them behind.

1.3.0 · 3 August 2026

  • Instant switching. Each app gets its own private group, so moving one in or out of the VPN is a firewall change rather than a restart. An app only restarts the first time it joins.
  • Firewall safety: rules are validated before they load, and can only ever target VPNonly's own groups.

1.2.0 · 3 August 2026

  • The menu bar app: every app on your Mac, searchable, with a switch each.
  • One password prompt ever, instead of one per action.
