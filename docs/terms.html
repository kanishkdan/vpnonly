Privacy

VPNonly collects nothing. There is no account, no telemetry, no analytics inside the app, and no server of ours between you and the internet. Specifically:

  • Your WireGuard key is stored on your Mac, readable only by your user account, and is never transmitted anywhere except to your own VPN provider's servers.
  • VPNonly does not inspect, log, or route your traffic. It configures macOS to send chosen apps through a tunnel — the traffic itself goes to your VPN provider, exactly as it would with their own client.
  • The app contacts our servers for exactly two things: activating your licence key once, and a periodic check for a newer version. Neither sends anything about you or your usage.
  • Your VPN provider has its own privacy policy governing traffic that reaches it. That relationship is between you and them.

This website uses privacy-friendly page-view counting with no cookies and no personal data. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, who receive your billing details under their own privacy policy — we never see your card.

What VPNonly does and doesn't promise

VPNonly is a routing tool, not a security guarantee. It is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. It does what the How it works page describes, including its stated limitations — notably that DNS queries for tunneled apps currently use the system resolver. If anonymity is critical to your safety, use a purpose-built tool such as Tor, not a per-app router.
